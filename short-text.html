Short Text

Short Text Specification:

Storage Layout:
Each text character requires exactly 1 byte (8 bits) of space under basic ASCII mapping templates.
Maximum Field Size:
Supports configurations up to 255 characters
Note: For classroom demonstration purposes, the field size capacity is limited to a maximum of 25.
